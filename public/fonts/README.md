# Fonts

Self-hosted copies of the page's three typefaces, the Latin subsets as served by Google Fonts:

- DM Sans (variable weight, used at 400 and 500) and DM Mono (400 and 500) by Colophon Foundry,
  Jonny Pinhorn and Indian Type Foundry: https://github.com/googlefonts/dm-fonts
- Instrument Serif (regular and italic) by Rodrigo Fuenzalida and Jordan Egstad:
  https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-serif

All three are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt). Hosting them here saves the
two extra connections and the stylesheet round trip of loading them from Google Fonts.
