# Artwork sources and quality notes

The visible ten-album collection uses official digital fronts from Apple Music at 1200 pixels. Printed CD faces, back inserts, and booklet scans come from the community-run [Cover Art Archive](https://coverartarchive.org/) via MusicBrainz. The exact image links for the five new albums are in [`public/bands/favorites/band.json`](public/bands/favorites/band.json) under `sources`; the five earlier albums reuse the scans in their original artist folders.

All ten CD faces measure roughly 1200 × 1200 pixels. A centred, slight inset is applied when mapping the circular scans to the 3D discs, hiding scanner background at the cut edge. Booklet spreads are split into square pages in memory so the archived files stay unmodified. The page sequence follows the archive's order where it is clear; some releases do not label every page, so the viewer is a visual demo rather than a definitive reconstruction of the original pagination.
