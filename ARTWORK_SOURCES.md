# Artwork sources and quality notes

The visible ten-album collection uses official digital fronts from Apple Music at 1200 pixels. Printed CD faces, back inserts, and most booklet scans come from the community-run [Cover Art Archive](https://coverartarchive.org/) via MusicBrainz. Coldplay's *X&Y* uses the original digital booklet PDF for its interiors. Exact source links are recorded in [`public/bands/favorites/band.json`](public/bands/favorites/band.json) under `sources`; earlier albums reuse the scans in their original artist folders.

All ten CD faces measure roughly 1200 × 1200 pixels. A centred, slight inset is applied when mapping the circular scans to the 3D discs, hiding scanner background at the cut edge. Scanned booklet spreads are split into square pages in memory so the archived files stay unmodified. The page sequence follows the archive's order where it is clear; some releases do not label every page, so the viewer is a visual demo rather than a definitive reconstruction of the original pagination.

## Moon Safari booklet (30 October 2026)

The user approved *Moon Safari* as Silent Shout's replacement. The selected [1998 French CD](https://musicbrainz.org/release/4c55906c-349b-362d-922e-956762912b42) is one disc with ten tracks, Source/Virgin CDV 2848 / 724384497828. Its complete archive sequence identifies pages 1–12; visible centre staples establish its bound construction. A [separately documented pressing](https://www.musik-sammler.de/release/air-moon-safari-cd-1045496/) also explicitly describes a 12-page stapled booklet.

Five interior spreads are retained at their original archive dimensions (2853–2856 × 1392–1398 pixels), unchanged on disk, and split into pages by the existing viewer. The left half of the outer scan supplies page 12; the matching digital Apple cover supplies page 1. The CD face is centred and resized to 1200 pixels, and the rear insert retains both spines. All ten Apple track IDs were checked against the CD's track order. The other nine collection entries are unchanged.

Follow-up verification: this is the complete twelve-page sequence. The source prints five lyric sections, credits for all ten songs, and the complete tracklist on page 12. Moon Safari uses `bookletFit: contain` to retain each full half-spread: the old square crop clipped the centre edges and interrupted illustrations. Its Austrian Apple IDs now explicitly select the Austrian preview lookup and store link; actual advancing playback of all ten previews was verified on desktop and mobile.

These interiors **are scans of printed paper**. [Qobuz lists a digital booklet](https://www.qobuz.com/at-de/album/moon-safari-air/0724384497859), but no original digital file was obtained or used. The generated retailer catalogue PDFs found elsewhere are not substitutes for the designed album booklet. Exact provenance, source dimensions, checksums, Apple metadata and binding evidence are in `research/moon-safari-replacement-2026-10-30/`.

## X&Y digital booklet (30 October 2026)

The [Coldplaying digital-booklet discussion](https://coldplaying.com/forums/topic/65224-coldplay-itunes-digital-booklets/) links the public PDF mirror used here. The downloaded file has eight landscape pages, embedded 1650-pixel-wide RGB images, and Adobe Photoshop CS creation metadata dated May 2005. Its clean digital layouts differ from the printed CD pagination. The untouched PDF, SHA-256, metadata, and overview are preserved in `research/album-replacement-2026-10-30/`.

The viewer follows those **eight digital pages**, with the matching official square cover, six interior layouts (PDF pages 2–7), and the digital final graphic/credits page. The landscape layouts are fitted into square images by extending their flat background colour above and below; no photographs or type are cropped or stretched. These are digital source images, not retouched paper scans. This square adaptation is for the demo and is not presented as an exact reproduction of the printed pagination.

The physical format also meets the selection criteria: [a documented single-CD pressing has a 12-page stapled booklet](https://www.musik-sammler.de/release/coldplay-x-y-cd-719123/). The [European edition's complete page scans](https://musicbrainz.org/release/a45b3f71-8ff1-4498-8a7a-e2d18a2f6aa8/cover-art) were checked only as a format/reference source. They are not used for the new booklet's interiors. The player uses the thirteen tracks of that single CD, including “Til Kingdom Come”.
